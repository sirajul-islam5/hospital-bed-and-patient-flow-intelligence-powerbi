# 🏥 Hospital Bed & Patient Flow Intelligence — Power BI Project

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze hospital operations, patient flow, bed occupancy, and wait time trends across departments and admission types. This is a self-made project designed to apply and demonstrate my data analytics skills in a real-world healthcare context. 

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Hospitals deal with enormous amounts of operational data every day — patient admissions, bed availability, wait times, and department loads. This dashboard was built to make sense of that data and answer the questions that hospital administrators genuinely need answered: Which wards are overloaded? Are wait times getting worse? Which departments keep patients the longest?

The dataset covers patient records across 6 departments, 6 age groups, 3 admission types, and multiple diagnosis categories — giving a comprehensive picture of hospital performance across a full year. 

---

## 📊 Dashboard Sections

**1. Are Wait Times Getting Worse Over the Year?**
- Line chart tracking average patient wait times month by month from January through December
- Wait times fluctuate between 7 and 13, with a noticeable peak around August–September
- Helps identify periods of operational stress and seasonal pressure

**2. What's the Average Length of Stay by Department?**
- Horizontal bar chart comparing average length of stay across all departments
- Gives a clear picture of which departments hold patients longer and may need additional resources or process review

**3. Hospital Admissions by Category**
- Donut chart breaking down admissions by type: Planned (43% — 87 admissions), Referral (36.5% — 73), and Emergency (20% — 40)
- Planned admissions dominate, but Emergency and Referral together account for the majority — a key operational insight

**4. Beds Occupied by Diagnosis**
- Treemap visual showing bed occupancy distribution across diagnosis categories
- Fracture occupies the largest share, followed by Diabetes Complication, Cardiac Arrest, Accidental Injury, Respiratory issues, Fever/Infections, Maternity, and Appendicitis
- Useful for understanding which conditions drive the most bed demand at any given time

**5. Which Ward is Most Overloaded?**
- Bar chart comparing patient load across all wards: General Ward A, General Ward B, Emergency Ward, Maternity Ward, Pediatric Ward, and ICU
- General Ward A and B carry the heaviest loads, approaching 1K patients — a clear signal for resource allocation decisions

## 🎛️ Interactive Slicers / Filters

| Slicer | Options |
|--------|---------|
| Age Group | 0–12, 13–17, 18–40, 41–60, 61–80, 80+ |
| Department | Cardiology, Emergency, General Medicine, Obstetrics, Orthopedics, Pediatrics |
| Admission Type | Emergency, Planned, Referral |

All visuals update dynamically based on slicer selections, allowing for deep drill-down analysis by patient demographic, department, or admission category.
